California

Ammunition

Ammunition purchases require an FFL or authorized credentials.

  • All ammunition must be shipped to a licensed FFL dealer
  • Alternatively, the buyer may present an Ammunition Vendor License, Type 03 FFL with Certificate of Eligibility, or proof of active law enforcement or military status
  • Law enforcement and military agencies must supply a purchase order on official letterhead
  • .50 BMG ammunition is prohibited
  • Local ammo restrictions: Los Angeles City, Oakland City, Sacramento City, San Francisco County/City, and Avalon City prohibit ammunition and projectile shipments

Firearms

Strict compliance requirements for handguns, rifles, and shotguns.

  • All handguns must appear on the California Approved Handgun Roster (single-action revolvers with 3"+ barrel and 7.5"+ overall length are exempt)
  • Non-compliant semi-automatic centerfire rifles and shotguns classified as assault weapons are restricted to law enforcement, military, and dealers holding an FFL with Dangerous Weapons permit
  • NFA items including suppressors, short-barreled rifles, short-barreled shotguns, and machine guns are restricted to law enforcement and military
  • Suppressors are prohibited for civilian sale
  • Complete upper receivers with barrels shorter than 16 inches are not available
  • Threaded pistol barrels are not available

Frames & Receivers

Unserialized frames and receivers are prohibited.

  • We do not ship 80% lowers, unserialized frames, or unserialized receivers to California
  • Complete lower receivers are not available for shipment
  • All barrels and upper assemblies must be shipped to an FFL holder — buyer must present government-issued photo ID with name, date of birth, and address

Magazines

Maximum capacity of 10 rounds.

  • Magazines holding more than 10 rounds are prohibited (tubular .22 LR magazines and tubular lever-action magazines are exempt)
  • This includes any parts or kits that could convert a magazine to hold more than 10 rounds
  • Exemptions require proof of active law enforcement status, exempt organization permit, assault weapon possession permit, or FFL with Large Capacity Magazine Permit

Parts & Accessories

Restrictions on specific components and accessories.

  • Binary triggers are prohibited
  • Certain parts and tools require government-issued photo ID before the order will be processed
  • Restricted item orders ship via USPS only — buyer must be present to show ID and sign at delivery
  • Holders of an FFL or Ammunition Vendor License may provide it instead of personal identification

Knives

Automatic knife restrictions apply statewide.

  • Automatic knives and switchblades are not available for shipment

Other Restrictions

Additional product restrictions apply.

  • Tannerite and binary exploding targets are prohibited
  • Flamethrowers are prohibited
  • Pepper sprays, pepper gels, pepper balls, and pepper guns are not available
  • All airguns must be equipped with an orange tip

  • We do not ship 80% lowers, unserialized frames, or unserialized receivers to Washington
  • Completed and stripped lowers are not available for shipment
  • Parts kits, build kits, rifle kits, pistol kits, and essential kits are not available

Magazines

Strict capacity limits apply.

  • Maximum capacity of 10 rounds (tubular .22 rimfire and lever-action tubular magazines are exempt)
  • Shotgun magazines are limited to 7 rounds
  • Exemptions require a law enforcement or military department purchase order, or FFL with department purchase order request

Parts & Accessories

Certain trigger components are restricted.

  • Binary triggers are not available for shipment

Knives

Automatic knife restrictions apply.

  • Automatic knives and switchblades are not available for shipment

Other Restrictions

Airgun requirements apply.

  • All airguns must be equipped with an orange tip
